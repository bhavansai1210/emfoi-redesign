# Current EMFOI Website Parity Audit

Audit target: https://www.emfoi.com/

Initial browser findings: the live homepage identifies itself as “IT Services - EMFOI”. The sandbox browser did not expose interactive elements or a clean screenshot, so the page HTML was saved for passive source extraction. No redesign changes have been made from this initial observation.

The next audit step is to extract visible navigation labels, page links, service terms, industry terms, products, contact details, proof points, and calls to action from the saved HTML, then cross-check each item against the redesign.

## Live homepage inventory from public extraction

The present homepage positions EMFOI as an IT-services partner with the message “Make us your IT team, so you can manage your business,” a free-consultation CTA, and a service promise focused on secure, scalable business continuity. It exposes four proof metrics: 10 years, 98% customer satisfaction, 15 completed projects, and 1-day email response. These are current-site claims and should not be carried into the redesign unless the user confirms they remain approved.

The current service taxonomy includes UI/UX Services, IT Consulting & Advisory, IT Modernization, Web Development, Mobile Development, AI/ML Development Services, Managed Services, Cyber Security, Cloud Services, Network Connectivity, and ERP Solutions. The redesign already represents several of these under software and AI capabilities, but the full taxonomy needs a parity check, especially cyber security, cloud services, network connectivity, ERP, managed services, and IT modernization.

The current industry taxonomy includes Industry & Manufacturing, Startups, Healthcare, Government, Consulting Providers, and Non-Profit, with additional linked labels that appear mismatched in the extracted page (for example, “Startups” linking to transportation/logistics and “Government” linking to banks/insurance). The redesign already represents Government, Healthcare, Manufacturing, and Financial Services, but should be checked for Startups, Consulting Providers, Non-Profit, and Transportation/Logistics coverage.

The current vendor/platform section names Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, Custom Development Solutions, Network & Connectivity, Cisco, Sophos, WatchGuard, Extreme Networks, Dell Technologies, VMware, Veeam, Altaro, Acronis, and Zerto. The redesign should preserve only verified partner/platform relationships and should not imply approval without confirmation.

The current contact flow includes info@emfoi.com, a phone contact, a Herndon, Virginia address, a consultation form with service options, a three-step “what happens next” process, and benefits described as client-oriented, independent, competent, results-driven, problem-solving, and transparent. The redesign includes a contact route but should be checked for the service-option coverage and the three-step inquiry process.

The current footer links include About us, Why us, Careers, Solutions, Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy, LinkedIn, Github, Twitter, and Facebook. The redesign has About, Careers, Privacy, and social links; “Why us,” Terms & Conditions, GitHub, and the current products/solutions footer taxonomy require explicit parity decisions.

Current product evidence includes DoFiling via dofiling.com and a section titled Our Products. The redesign includes DoFiling and CareHigh, but CareHigh is a newer user-requested addition rather than a current-site item.

## Redesign-side cross-check

The redesign has routes for Capabilities, Government, Healthcare, Industries, Financial Services, Manufacturing, About, DoFiling, CareHigh, Careers, Contact, Privacy, Insights, Capability Statement, and a 404 fallback. Its source includes software, UI/UX, AI governance, IT consulting, staffing, cloud, ERP, Government, Healthcare, Financial Services, and Manufacturing terms, plus the requested LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, and X footer channels.

The parity gap is not a simple missing-page problem. The current site’s broader legacy service taxonomy includes Cyber Security, Managed Services, Network Connectivity, Web Development, Mobile Development, Cloud Services, ERP Solutions, and IT Modernization; some are represented only indirectly or not visibly as dedicated destinations in the redesign. The current site’s broader industry taxonomy includes Startups, Consulting Providers, Non-Profit, and Transportation/Logistics; these are not clearly represented in the redesign’s primary industries ledger.

The current site also presents proof metrics, vendor/platform names, a three-step consultation flow, “Why us,” Terms & Conditions, GitHub, and a direct DoFiling link. These should be treated as parity candidates, not automatically copied: metrics and partner claims require user approval, while legal and navigation links can be preserved if the user confirms the current URLs.

## Approved integration findings

The user approved carrying forward verified service and industry categories, Mission, core values, the Why Us framework, the contact process, current contact details, and legal navigation. Metrics, employee counts, awards, and vendor or partner claims remain excluded until separately confirmed.

The approved service routes now include Cyber Security, Managed Services, Cloud Services, Network Connectivity, ERP Solutions, and IT Modernization. Web and mobile development are represented within Custom Software & UX; AI/ML and software development are represented within the AI and custom software practices.

The approved industry routes now include Food, Startups, Consulting Providers, Non-Profit, AI, and Other, in addition to the existing Healthcare, Government, Financial Services & Insurance, and Manufacturing routes. The legacy extraction contained inconsistent links for some industry labels, so the redesign uses the visible category names with newly written, non-claim-based context rather than copying those mismatched destinations. Transportation/Logistics was not added because it was not a clean, reliable category in the extracted taxonomy.

The approved About update adds a mission centered on pairing personal service with practical technology and core values adapted from the visible legacy language: Passionate, Respectful, Ownership, and Unified. The existing role-based team presentation remains illustrative and does not introduce fabricated biographies.

## Legal parity verification

Source: https://www.emfoi.com/terms-conditions/ (retrieved 2026-08-26). The current page is a WordPress-generated Terms & Conditions shell containing “Suggested text” privacy, cookie, comment, and media language rather than a bespoke legal agreement. The redesign should therefore provide an accessible local legal route without presenting the old boilerplate as approved EMFOI legal terms, and should link to the current source only as a reference if needed.
